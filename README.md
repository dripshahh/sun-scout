# ☀️ Sun Scout

A free sun-and-light planner for cinematographers and photographers.

- **Map:** sun path for any place and date, sunrise / sunset / golden and blue hour, live Meteosat clouds, rain radar.
- **"Right now, from space":** checks the latest satellite image for cloud over your exact spot.
- **3D and street view:** OpenStreetMap buildings, temples, trees detected from satellite photos, real sun shadows, lens choices (16–85 mm).
- **Hills:** works out when the sun actually clears or drops behind terrain.
- **Fix the map:** click any building to correct its type or height; save and share your fixes as a file.
- Works on phones and installs to the home screen.

Data: OpenStreetMap (via OpenFreeMap, Photon, Nominatim), Open-Meteo, EUMETSAT, RainViewer, Esri World Imagery, AWS Terrain Tiles, SunCalc.
